# Contributing to VetEscape

_Last updated: November 8, 2026_

Thank you for helping veterans and their families find useful outdoor experiences and support. These guidelines apply to resource suggestions, discussions, issues, and code contributions. They do not promise publication or a response.

## Suggest a resource

Use the homepage form or GitHub Discussions. The form prepares a public GitHub issue draft and sends its fields to GitHub to prefill it; it does not publish an issue until you submit it there. Published issues and discussions are public. Include only information appropriate for a public website.

Where available, include:

- Program or place name and official website.
- Location or service area.
- What it offers and who it serves; include eligibility only if the provider publishes it.
- Current cost, accessibility, family participation, and reservation details from an authoritative source.
- The date you checked the details.

### Resource review standards

- Prefer the provider's official site, government land manager, or another primary source.
- Disclose if you own, work for, or represent the provider.
- Do not make unsupported claims about treatment, clinical outcomes, safety, discounts, eligibility, or free services.
- Do not present a listing as a replacement for crisis or professional care.
- Maintainers may verify links, request sources, decline a submission, or remove outdated or unsafe material. Inclusion is not an endorsement, guarantee, referral, or clinical judgment.
- Report broken links or material changes with an authoritative source and date checked.

## Protect people and privacy

Never include medical or mental-health details, crisis information, personal phone numbers, private email addresses, financial details, precise home locations, or identifying information about yourself or anyone else. Do not share information without permission. Public GitHub content may be copied, indexed, emailed, or retained in platform history after edits or deletion. Read [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

Be respectful. Harassment, threats, discrimination, spam, impersonation, doxxing, and unlawful content are not permitted. Maintainers may hide or remove content and restrict participation where platform tools allow.

## Contribute code

For substantial changes, open an issue first. Pull requests should explain the problem and change, preserve mobile and keyboard accessibility and crisis notices, include manual test steps, and avoid credentials, private data, and unlicensed material. Do not introduce analytics, trackers, sensitive-data collection, or new third-party services without documenting them and updating privacy disclosures. Test mobile layouts, forms, keyboard use, and offline behavior as relevant.

Submitting a pull request does not guarantee acceptance. Check the repository for a license before reusing code; no license means no permission is granted by these guidelines. Do not submit content or code unless you have the right to do so. No separate copyright assignment is requested here; GitHub terms and any repository license still apply.

## Health, safety, and independence

VetEscape is a directory, not a medical provider, booking service, emergency service, or land manager. Do not portray recreation as a substitute for therapy or crisis care. Providers are responsible for their own services, eligibility, accessibility, and safety details. Distinguish verifiable facts from opinions.

Sponsorship does not buy inclusion, ranking, or recommendations. Disclose financial or personal relationships to maintainers and clearly label material relationships if published. Commercial relationships must never influence crisis or clinical information.

## Maintainer review

Maintainers may edit for clarity, ask for sources, decline or remove submissions, and revise these guidelines. They cannot guarantee every listing is current or every suggestion receives a reply. Raise concerns privately through the repository maintainer contact; do not put sensitive details in public issues.

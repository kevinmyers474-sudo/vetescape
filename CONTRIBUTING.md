# Contributing to VetEscape

Thank you for helping veterans and their families discover safe, useful outdoor experiences and support.

## Suggest a resource

Start a topic in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/kevinmyers474-sudo/vetescape/discussions) with:

- Program or place name and official website.
- Location or service area.
- What it offers and who is eligible (if known).
- Cost, accessibility, and family participation details, when published by the provider.
- The date you last checked the information.

Please use official provider information, avoid unsupported medical claims, and never post anyone’s private health, financial, or identifying information. Submissions are suggestions, not endorsements; maintainers may verify, edit, or decline them.

## Contribute code

Open an issue to describe a bug or improvement, then submit a pull request. Keep the site accessible, mobile-friendly, privacy-preserving, and usable without an account. Do not add trackers, collect sensitive data, or make paid placement look like an independent recommendation.

## Safety and independence

VetEscape is a directory, not a medical provider, booking service, or emergency service. Do not present outdoor recreation as a substitute for therapy. Keep crisis contacts prominent and verify resource links before adding or updating them. Disclose sponsorships or affiliate relationships clearly; financial support must not influence clinical information or emergency resources.

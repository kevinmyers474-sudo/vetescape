# Contributing to VetEscape

Thank you for helping veterans and their families find useful outdoor experiences and support.

## Suggest a resource

Use the homepage form. It prepares a GitHub issue for you to review and submit; the form itself does not send or store your suggestion. Published issues are public and managed by GitHub. You may instead open a topic in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/kevinmyers474-sudo/vetescape/discussions).

Useful information to include:

- Program or place name and official website.
- Location or service area.
- What it offers and who is eligible, if the provider publishes that information.
- Cost, accessibility, and family participation details, where available.
- When you last checked the information.

Use official provider information, avoid unsupported medical claims, and never include anyone’s private health, financial, contact, or identifying information. Submissions are suggestions, not endorsements. Maintainers may verify, edit, decline, or remove listings. Check that the program is active and links work before proposing a change.

## Contribute code

Open an issue to describe a bug or improvement, then submit a pull request. Keep the site accessible, mobile-friendly, privacy-preserving, and usable without an account. Do not add trackers, collect sensitive data, or make paid placement look like an independent recommendation. Test your change on a small screen and with keyboard navigation.

## Safety, sponsorship, and independence

VetEscape is a directory, not a medical provider, booking service, or emergency service. Do not present outdoor recreation as a substitute for therapy. Keep crisis contacts prominent and verify resource links. Sponsorship does not buy a recommendation or placement. Clearly disclose any future paid placements or affiliate links, and never let a financial relationship influence clinical or crisis information.

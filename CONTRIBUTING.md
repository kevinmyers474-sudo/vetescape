# Contributing to VetEscape

_Last updated: November 8, 2026_

Thank you for helping veterans and their families find useful outdoor experiences and support. These guidelines apply to resource suggestions, discussions, issues, and code contributions. They are community rules, not a promise that a suggestion will be published or that maintainers can provide a response.

## 1. Suggest a resource

Use the homepage form or open a GitHub Discussion. The form prepares a public GitHub issue draft; it transmits the fields to GitHub to prefill the draft, but does not publish an issue until you submit it there. Published issues and discussions are public. Include only information appropriate for a public website.

Please provide, where available:

- Program or place name and its official website.
- Location or service area.
- What it offers and who it serves; include eligibility only when published by the provider.
- Cost, accessibility, family participation, and reservation details from a current official source.
- The date you checked the details.

### Resource review standards

- Prefer the provider’s official site, government land manager, or another primary source.
- Do not submit a provider you own or represent without disclosing that relationship.
- Do not make unsupported claims about treatment, clinical outcomes, safety, discounts, eligibility, or “free” services.
- Do not submit a listing as a substitute for crisis or professional care.
- Maintainers may verify links and details, request clarification, decline a submission, or remove outdated or unsafe material. Inclusion is not an endorsement, guarantee, referral, or clinical judgment.
- Report broken links or material changes with a source and the date checked.

## 2. Protect people and privacy

Never include medical or mental-health details, crisis information, personal phone numbers, private email addresses, financial details, precise home locations, or identifying information about yourself or another person. Do not share information without permission. Public GitHub content may be copied, indexed, emailed, or retained in platform history even if later edited or deleted. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

Be respectful. Harassment, threats, discrimination, spam, impersonation, doxxing, and unlawful content are not permitted. Maintainers may hide or remove content and restrict participation where platform tools allow.

## 3. Contribute code

For a substantial change, open an issue first so the approach can be discussed. Pull requests should:

- Explain the user problem and proposed change.
- Keep the site responsive, keyboard accessible, and understandable to screen readers.
- Preserve prominent crisis links and clear medical, outdoor-safety, and sponsor disclosures.
- Avoid collecting sensitive information, adding analytics or trackers, or introducing new third-party services without documenting them and updating the privacy statement.
- Include manual test steps. Check mobile layout, forms, keyboard use, and offline behavior when relevant.
- Avoid including credentials, private data, or copyrighted material you do not have permission to use.

Submitting a pull request does not guarantee acceptance. Check the repository for a license before reusing its code; absence of a license does not grant permission to reuse it. Do not submit code or content unless you have the right to do so. No separate copyright assignment is requested by these guidelines; any contribution terms specified by GitHub or a repository license still apply.

## 4. Health, safety, and independence

VetEscape is a directory, not a medical provider, booking service, emergency service, or land manager. Do not portray outdoor recreation as a replacement for therapy or crisis care. Providers are responsible for their own services, eligibility, accessibility, and safety information. Clearly distinguish factual resource details from opinions.

Sponsorship does not buy inclusion, ranking, or a recommendation. Any paid, affiliate, or personal relationship must be disclosed to maintainers and clearly labeled to users if published. Commercial relationships must never influence crisis or clinical information.

## 5. Maintainer review

Maintainers may edit for clarity, ask for sources, decline or remove submissions, and change these guidelines. They cannot guarantee every listing is current or every suggestion receives a reply. To raise a concern privately, contact the maintainers through the repository profile; do not put sensitive details in a public issue.

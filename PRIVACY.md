# VetEscape Privacy Statement

_Last updated: November 8, 2026_

This statement describes the current VetEscape static website and installable web app. It is a plain-language description, not legal advice. The site owner should have it reviewed for applicable privacy laws and update it whenever hosting, functionality, or third-party services change.

## At a glance

- VetEscape does not currently have accounts, a VetEscape-operated submission server, analytics, advertising trackers, or on-site payment processing.
- The app stores its static app-shell files in your browser using a service worker so previously visited pages can work offline. You can clear this data through your browser’s site-data settings.
- The resource suggestion form puts the information you enter into a URL that opens a GitHub issue draft. This transmits the draft details to GitHub when the page opens; nothing is published as an issue until you review and submit it on GitHub.
- The location search sends the location you enter to Google Maps when you choose to open the map search. VetEscape does not receive or store the map search.
- The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google to retrieve those font files; Google may receive technical information such as your IP address under its own policies.

## Information and technologies used by VetEscape

VetEscape does not currently operate a login, user profile, database, server-side form endpoint, analytics service, advertising tracker, or payment processor. Searching and filtering the directory happen in your browser. The service worker caches static site files on your device for the app experience and offline loading; the cache is not a VetEscape account or server-side record.

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive technical request information and process it under its own policies and terms. Your browser and network provider may also process ordinary connection data. VetEscape does not control those systems or their retention practices.

## Resource suggestions and public contributions

The resource form builds a GitHub issue-draft URL from the form fields and opens that URL. As a result, field contents are sent to GitHub to populate the draft, and may appear in browser history or other browser features. The form does not send the information to a VetEscape-operated server. Nothing is publicly posted until you review and submit the issue on GitHub. If you publish it, the issue and its contents are public and handled under GitHub’s terms and privacy policies. Discussions, issues, and pull requests are also public contribution channels.

Do not include medical or mental-health information, crisis details, private contact information, financial information, or identifying information about yourself or another person. If you need removal help, contact GitHub for its platform processes and the maintainers through the repository; deletion from the site may not remove copies, notifications, or Git history.

## Location search and external links

The location search uses the text you enter to open a Google Maps search in a new tab. VetEscape does not use browser geolocation or store the location. Google Maps and linked resource providers are independent services with their own privacy notices, terms, and data practices. Review those notices before using them.

## Fonts

The stylesheet requests fonts from Google Fonts. This means your browser makes a request to Google when the font is needed. If you prefer not to make that request, you can use browser settings or block the external font request; a system-font fallback is provided.

## Sponsorship and payments

The optional sponsorship link takes you to GitHub Sponsors. If you choose to contribute, GitHub and any payment providers it uses handle the transaction under their own terms and privacy policies. VetEscape does not receive or store card details through this website. Check the provider’s information for details about payment data, receipts, recurring contributions, and cancellation.

## Children and sensitive information

The site is a general information directory and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not use public contribution channels to submit sensitive information. VetEscape is not an emergency or clinical service.

## Changes and contact

This statement may be updated when the app or its providers change. For privacy questions or requests concerning VetEscape content, contact the maintainers through [the repository](https://github.com/kevinmyers474-sudo/vetescape). Do not post private information in public issues. This policy does not create rights beyond those provided by applicable law.

## Important for launch

This statement describes the implementation as currently understood and is not a legal compliance certification. Before launch, confirm the hosting and third-party services actually enabled, the location where the site owner operates, and any legal notice or contact requirements that apply. Obtain qualified legal advice if required, especially before adding accounts, email collection, analytics, advertising, direct donations, or a server-side submission flow.

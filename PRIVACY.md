# VetEscape Privacy Statement

_Last updated: November 8, 2026_

VetEscape is a static resource directory for veterans, service members, and their families. This statement describes the current website; it is not legal advice. Review it whenever hosting, forms, or services change.

## Information VetEscape collects

The current site has no VetEscape account system, server-side submission endpoint, analytics, advertising trackers, or payment processing. Resource search and category filters run in your browser. The service worker stores the site's app-shell files in your browser for offline access. The location search uses the place you enter to open Google Maps; VetEscape does not save that location or receive the map search. Your browser, hosting provider, GitHub, and other external services may process technical data under their own privacy policies.

## Resource suggestions and public contributions

The homepage form creates a draft GitHub issue in your browser and opens GitHub. Nothing is submitted until you review and publish it. If you publish, the issue and the information you include are public and processed under GitHub's privacy terms. Do not include medical or mental-health details, personal contact information, financial data, or anyone else's identifying information. GitHub Discussions, issues, and pull requests are public contribution channels.

## External links and sponsorship

Links to maps, resource providers, GitHub, and GitHub Sponsors take you to third-party services. Their privacy policies and terms apply when you use them. Optional sponsorship payments, if enabled, are processed by GitHub; VetEscape does not collect card details or donor information through this site. Check GitHub's terms for information about its payment and data practices.

## Children and sensitive information

This site is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not submit health, crisis, or other sensitive personal information to public forms, issues, or discussions. The site is not a crisis-response service.

## Changes and contact

This statement may be updated when site features or third-party services change. For privacy questions or removal requests concerning a public contribution, contact the repository maintainers through [GitHub](https://github.com/kevinmyers474-sudo/vetescape). Public content may remain in GitHub history or copies after deletion; consult GitHub for its data-removal process.

## Important note

This plain-language statement describes the current static site and is not a substitute for legal review. Before adding accounts, server-side forms, analytics, advertising, or other data collection, publish an updated policy and obtain appropriate legal guidance.

# VetEscape Privacy Statement

_Last updated: November 8, 2026_

This statement describes the current VetEscape static website and installable web app. It is a plain-language description, not legal advice or a compliance certification. The owner should review it against actual hosting, services, and applicable law before launch and update it when they change.

## At a glance

- VetEscape currently has no accounts, VetEscape-operated submission server, analytics, advertising trackers, or on-site payment processing.
- The service worker caches static app files in your browser for offline access.
- The resource form sends the fields you enter to GitHub in a URL to prefill an issue draft. Nothing is published until you review and submit it on GitHub.
- Choosing a location search sends the entered location to Google Maps.
- The stylesheet requests fonts from Google Fonts; your browser contacts Google to retrieve the fonts.

## Information and app storage

Directory search and filters run in your browser. The service worker stores the site's app-shell files locally so previously visited pages can load offline. This is local browser storage, not a VetEscape account or server-side record. You can clear it through your browser's site-data settings.

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive technical request information and process it under its own policies and terms. Your browser and network provider may also process ordinary connection data. VetEscape does not control their systems or retention practices.

## Resource suggestions and public contributions

The form builds a GitHub issue-draft URL from the form fields and opens GitHub. Field contents are sent to GitHub to prefill the draft and may appear in browser history or browser features. The form does not send them to a VetEscape-operated server. Nothing is posted as an issue until you review and submit it. If you publish it, its contents are public and processed under GitHub's terms and privacy policies. Discussions, issues, and pull requests are public contribution channels.

Do not include medical or mental-health information, crisis details, private contact information, financial information, or identifying information about yourself or another person. Contact GitHub about its platform removal processes and maintainers through [the repository](https://github.com/kevinmyers474-sudo/vetescape) about VetEscape content. Deletion may not remove copies, notifications, or Git history.

## Location search and external links

The location search uses the text you enter to open Google Maps in a new tab. VetEscape does not use browser geolocation or store that text. Google Maps and linked providers are independent services with their own privacy notices, terms, and data practices. Review those notices before using them.

## Fonts

The stylesheet requests fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google when fonts are needed; Google may receive technical information such as your IP address under its policies. System-font fallbacks are provided if the request is blocked.

## Sponsorship and payments

The optional support link takes you to GitHub Sponsors. If you contribute, GitHub and any payment providers it uses handle the transaction under their own terms and privacy policies. VetEscape does not receive or store card details through this website. Refer to the provider for payment data, receipts, recurring contributions, and cancellation information.

## Children and sensitive information

The site is a general information directory and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not use public contribution channels to submit sensitive information. VetEscape is not a clinical or emergency service.

## Changes and contact

This statement may be updated when the app or its providers change. For privacy questions about VetEscape content, contact maintainers through [the repository](https://github.com/kevinmyers474-sudo/vetescape). Do not post private information in public issues.

Before adding accounts, email collection, analytics, advertising, direct donations, or server-side submissions, update this statement and obtain appropriate legal guidance.

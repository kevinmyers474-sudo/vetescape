Privacy and trust

Simple rules. Clear data handling. No surprises.

VetEscape is intentionally minimal. This policy describes what the app does today, what it does not do, and how public contributions work.

What we do not collect

VetEscape does not currently run an account system, login flow, user database, server-side form processor, ad network, or payment system. The site does not require personal information to browse the resource guide.

What happens in the app

Searches, category filters, and the app shell run in the browser. The location search opens a map provider with the place you enter; VetEscape does not save the location you type. State may still be processed by your browser, hosting provider, or the map service under their own policies.

Public suggestions

The resource suggestion form opens a GitHub issue for you to review and submit. Anything you publish there becomes public on GitHub. Do not include medical details or personal identifying information. GitHub Discussions, issues, and pull requests are public contribution channels.

External services

Links to maps, resources, GitHub, and GitHub Sponsors route to third-party services. Their privacy policies and terms apply when you use them. Optional sponsorship is handled by GitHub and not by VetEscape directly.

Future changes

Before adding analytics, accounts, data collection, or paid services, VetEscape will update this policy and disclose the change clearly. This page is not legal advice.

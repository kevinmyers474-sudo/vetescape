Privacy and trust

Clear information about what happens when you use VetEscape.

This policy describes the current static website and installable web app, including its offline cache and external services.

Last updated: November 8, 2026. This plain-language statement describes the current implementation; it is not legal advice or a compliance certification. The owner should review it for applicable laws and actual services before launch.

At a glance

  • No VetEscape account, server-side submission endpoint, analytics, advertising tracker, or on-site payment processing is currently used.
  • The service worker caches static app files in your browser for offline access.
  • The suggestion form sends the fields you enter to GitHub in a URL to prefill an issue draft; nothing is published until you submit it on GitHub.
  • Choosing a location search sends the entered location to Google Maps.
  • The site requests fonts from Google Fonts; your browser contacts Google to retrieve them.

Information and app storage

Directory search and filters run in your browser. The service worker stores the app shell locally so previously visited pages can load offline. This is browser storage, not a VetEscape account or server-side record. Clear it through your browser's site-data settings.

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive technical request information under its own policies and terms. Your browser and network provider may also process ordinary connection data. VetEscape does not control their systems or retention practices.

Resource suggestions and public contributions

The form builds a GitHub issue-draft URL from its fields and opens GitHub. Field contents are sent to GitHub to prefill the draft and may appear in browser history or browser features. The form does not send them to a VetEscape-operated server. Nothing is posted until you review and submit the issue. If published, the issue is public and handled under GitHub's terms and privacy policies. Discussions, issues, and pull requests are public channels.

Do not include medical or mental-health information, crisis details, private contact information, financial information, or identifying information about anyone. Contact GitHub about its platform removal processes and maintainers through the repository. Removing content may not remove copies, notifications, or Git history.

Location search and external links

The location search uses the text you enter to open Google Maps in a new tab. VetEscape does not use browser geolocation or store that text. Maps and linked providers are independent services with their own privacy notices, terms, and data practices.

Fonts

The stylesheet requests fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google when fonts are needed; Google may receive technical information such as your IP address under its policies. System-font fallbacks are available if the request is blocked.

Sponsorship and payments

The optional support link takes you to GitHub Sponsors. GitHub and any payment providers it uses handle contributions under their own terms and privacy policies. VetEscape does not receive or store card details through this site. Refer to the provider for payment data, receipts, recurring contributions, and cancellation details.

Children and sensitive information

The site is a general information directory and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not use public contribution channels for sensitive information. VetEscape is not a clinical or emergency service.

Changes and contact

This statement may change when the app or its providers change. For privacy questions about VetEscape content, contact maintainers through the repository. Do not post private information in public issues.

Before adding accounts, email collection, analytics, advertising, direct donations, or server-side submissions, update this statement and obtain appropriate legal guidance.

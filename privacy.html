Privacy and trust

Clear information about what happens when you use VetEscape.

This policy describes the current static website and installable web app, including its offline cache and external services.

Last updated: November 8, 2026. This plain-language statement describes the current implementation; it is not legal advice. The site owner should have it reviewed for applicable privacy laws and update it if the service changes.

At a glance

  • No VetEscape account, user database, server-side form processor, analytics, advertising tracker, or on-site payment processing is currently used.
  • The service worker caches static app files in your browser for offline access.
  • The suggestion form sends the fields you enter to GitHub in a URL to prefill a draft issue; nothing is published until you submit the issue on GitHub.
  • Choosing a location search sends the entered location to Google Maps.
  • The site requests fonts from Google Fonts, which receives the browser request under Google’s own policies.

Information and technologies used

Searches and category filters run in your browser. The service worker stores the site's app-shell files locally so previously visited app pages can load offline; it is not a VetEscape account or server-side record. You can clear the cache through your browser's site-data settings.

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive technical request information and process it under its own policies and terms. Your browser and network provider may also process ordinary connection data. VetEscape does not control those systems or their retention practices.

Resource suggestions and public contributions

The suggestion form builds a GitHub issue-draft URL from the form fields and opens that URL. Field contents are sent to GitHub to populate the draft, and may appear in browser history or other browser features. The form does not send them to a VetEscape-operated server. Nothing is publicly posted until you review and submit the issue on GitHub. If published, the issue and its contents are public and handled under GitHub's terms and privacy policies. Discussions, issues, and pull requests are also public channels.

Do not include medical or mental-health information, crisis details, private contact information, financial information, or identifying information about yourself or another person. To request removal help, contact GitHub for its platform processes and the maintainers through the repository. Removing content from the site may not remove copies, notifications, or Git history.

Location search and external links

The location search uses the text you enter to open a Google Maps search in a new tab. VetEscape does not use browser geolocation or store the location. Google Maps and linked resource providers are independent services with their own privacy notices, terms, and data practices. Review those notices before using them.

Fonts

The stylesheet requests fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google to retrieve font files when needed; Google may receive technical information such as your IP address under its own policies. A system-font fallback is provided if you block external fonts.

Sponsorship and payments

The optional sponsorship link takes you to GitHub Sponsors. If you contribute, GitHub and any payment providers it uses handle the transaction under their own terms and privacy policies. VetEscape does not receive or store card details through this site. Check the provider's information for payment data, receipts, recurring contributions, and cancellation details.

Children and sensitive information

The site is a general information directory and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not use public contribution channels to submit sensitive information. VetEscape is not an emergency or clinical service.

Changes and contact

This statement may be updated when the app or its providers change. For privacy questions or requests concerning VetEscape content, contact maintainers through the repository. Do not post private information in public issues.

This policy is not a legal compliance certification. Before adding accounts, email collection, analytics, advertising, direct donations, or a server-side submission flow, update the policy and obtain appropriate legal guidance.
